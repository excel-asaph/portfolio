_______   PROJECT DETAILS

Understanding the project.

About

Beevo is an autonomous AI CMO that takes over the entire branding process from a single voice conversation. It runs live competitor research, generates a brand identity, and deploys a landing page with six autonomous agents continuously optimizing it in the background. What used to take 6 weeks and cost $15,000+ now takes 6 minutes.

Problem Statement

Most founders spend more time on marketing than on building their product. Hiring a branding agency costs $15,000+ minimum and takes weeks of back-and-forth. Competitor research, A/B testing, and landing page optimization each require their own tools, their own expertise, and their own budget. For early-stage startups, the gap between an idea and a professional market presence is one of the hardest to close.

_______   KEY OBSERVATIONS

Who is struggling, and why.

Research context
Before building Beevo with the team, I spent time looking at the actual problem. Not the product problem, but the human one. Why were founders still paying agencies they couldn't afford, or launching with no brand at all? The pattern was clear: branding is a sequential process of interconnected decisions, and the tools available treat each of those decisions as someone else's problem. I wanted us to build something that owned the whole chain.
The closest product I found in this space was Pomelli, a Google Labs AI marketing tool. Pomelli is polished and genuinely useful for generating on-brand social assets and campaign ideas. But Pomelli is designed to assist a marketer. The distinction Beevo was built around is simpler: not assistance, but replacement. That shift (from tool to autonomous agent) is what every architectural decision in the system was built to support.
The Founder's Reality

Early-stage founders are simultaneously building product, managing operations, and trying to go to market. When I looked at how they approach branding, the pattern was consistent: they either spend $15,000+ on an agency they can't afford, struggle with five or more disconnected tools, or launch with zero visual identity and hope traction follows. The branding process itself (competitor research, color theory, typography, landing page copy) takes four to six weeks minimum and demands expertise most founders simply don't have.

The Marketer's Workflow

Professional marketers use SWOT analysis, competitor research, A/B testing, and continuous optimization, all of which require specific expertise and expensive tooling. The landing page alone involves copywriting, visual design, CTA testing, and conversion analysis. What became clear was that these aren't creative mysteries. They're systematic, data-driven processes. The question became whether a multi-agent system could replicate these workflows reliably enough to produce professional-grade output without human intervention at every step.

The Engineering Challenge

The core challenge was not AI capability. Gemini's models are powerful enough. The challenge was orchestration: how do you coordinate voice interaction, live competitor research, asset generation, and continuous page optimization without the system collapsing into conflicts, state inconsistencies, or response latency that breaks the conversation feel? The answer required a fundamentally different architecture. Not a single model trying to do everything, but a dual-model bridge that separates voice from execution, with a filesystem-based state layer that all agents share as a single source of truth.

Questions that drove the design
Every architectural decision in Beevo was driven by a specific question that emerged from watching the branding workflow up close. These weren't hypothetical. They were real friction points that required engineering solutions.
''
How do you run real-time voice conversation while simultaneously executing multi-step tool workflows, without either breaking?
''
How do 6 autonomous optimization agents share a live landing page without stepping on each other's state?
''
How do you generate AI marketing assets (logos, videos, copy) that are actually brand-consistent and not just aesthetically pleasing?
''
How do you keep a human in control of an autonomous system without forcing them to micromanage every decision?
''
Can a structured JSON schema replace generated HTML for producing reliable, renderable UI components from an LLM?
''
What does real-time competitor research look like when it must use live market data rather than static, pre-scraped databases?

ARCHITECTURE & SYSTEM

_______   THE BRIDGE ARCHITECTURE

The dual-model core.

Why two models?
The most fundamental challenge in Beevo was making real-time voice conversation and complex tool execution coexist. A single model trying to do both fails in two directions: tool calls interrupt the voice stream, and response latency breaks the conversation feel entirely. The solution was architectural: separate voice from execution completely. Gemini Live handles only the human conversation layer. Gemini Flash handles all tool decisions and execution. They communicate through a 3-second transcript buffer, creating a coordinated system where the user experiences a natural CMO conversation while a parallel engine executes the actual work.
Model 1
Gemini Live gemini-2.5-flash-native-audio-preview
Handles the real-time voice conversation. Listens, understands, responds. No tool calls, no latency spikes.
→ Native audio streaming (PCM 16kHz input / 24kHz output)
→ Voice Activity Detection (VAD)
→ Buffers transcripts every 3 seconds
→ Pauses during tool execution
→ Phase management: discovery → execution → modification
⟶
Model 2
Gemini Flash (Brain) gemini-3-flash-preview
Reads the transcript buffer, decides which tools to call, executes the entire brand-building workflow.
→ Receives buffered transcripts every 3 seconds
→ 20+ function declarations for brand building
→ Calls downstream agents and services
→ Emits execution logs to the canvas in real time
→ Signals Live to resume after tool completion
Design Philosophy
Every system decision in Beevo follows a single principle: autonomy with transparency. Agents act independently, but every decision is logged as a "Thought Signature", a visible reasoning chain on the brand canvas, so users understand exactly why the system proposed a change. This maintains trust in a system that most users have never experienced before: one that builds their brand and rewrites their website while they sleep.

_______   THE WATCHER AGENTS

6 agents. Continuous optimization.

Always watching, always improving
Once the landing page is generated, Beevo deploys six specialized Watcher Agents that run on a continuous cycle. Each agent owns one section of the landing page. They screenshot it with Puppeteer, analyze visual hierarchy with Gemini 3 Pro Vision, reason about optimization opportunities with Gemini Flash, then propose changes through the Human-in-the-Loop control system before staging anything to the live page.
H
Hero Watcher
Above-the-fold impact
Analyzes headline clarity, visual hierarchy, and CTA prominence. Proposes copy improvements and hero image variations based on scroll retention signals.
P
Proof Watcher
Trust & statistics
Monitors dwell time on the proof section. Generates new evidence items (stats, trust badges, dashboards) using the NanoBanana Design System when engagement drops.
O
Offer Watcher
Conversion optimization
Tests CTA button text, color, and position. Analyzes click-through rates and suggests pricing layout changes to reduce friction at the conversion point.
A
PAS Watcher
Messaging narrative
Monitors the Problem-Agitate-Solve messaging flow. Ensures emotional progression is maintained and refines the 3-step narrative based on scroll depth analytics.
S
Spec Watcher
Feature & product details
Tracks which features users engage with longest. Reorders and emphasizes high-engagement specs while suggesting updates to feature descriptions based on performance data.
W
Social Watcher
Social proof
Analyzes testimonial section performance and scroll-through rate. Suggests new testimonial content and sharing element positioning using Gemini image generation.

_______   HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

Autonomy with control.

The approval flow
Beevo never autonomously changes the live page without user awareness. Every Watcher Agent proposal goes through a configurable intervention system with real-time notifications via WebSocket, Telegram, and Web Push. Users can approve, reject, or modify proposals from any device. If they don't respond within a set timeout, the system auto-proceeds to keep optimization moving without blocking indefinitely.
1
Watcher detects an optimization opportunity
e.g. Hero headline engagement below threshold. Screenshots captured via Puppeteer. Vision analysis completed by Gemini 3 Pro.
2
Proposal generated (PRE or POST mode)
In PRE_GENERATION mode, Watcher asks approval before creating new content. In POST_GENERATION mode, it creates first, then asks approval to stage.
3
Multi-channel notification sent
HITL Control Center (WebSocket), Telegram Bot, and Web Push (VAPID) all fire simultaneously. Notification: "Hero Watcher: Change headline to 'Performance that heals the planet'?"
4
User responds, or auto-proceed fires
Approve → change stages to landing page. Reject → Watcher tries a different approach. No response after N minutes → auto-approved to keep optimization moving.
5
Live page updates via WebSocket
Staging JSON pushed to the landing page component. React re-renders the updated section with a skeleton loader transition. The change is live.

_______   THE GUARDIAN

Pixel-precise brand compliance.

Asset auditing with Gemini Vision
The Guardian is a dedicated auditing module powered by Gemini 3 Pro Vision. You upload any brand asset (a logo, campaign image, or social graphic), and it checks it against the Brand DNA's hex codes, safe zone rules, and visual guidelines, then returns bounding-box overlays pinpointing exactly where violations occur on the image. The coordinates are pixel-precise: [ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax] for every flag. Wrong color, logo distortion, safe zone intrusion, brand-inconsistent content. The Guardian finds it and shows you the exact pixel location.
C
Color Compliance
Cross-references every dominant color in the asset against Brand DNA hex codes. Flags mismatches with the exact pixel region and the expected vs. found value.
L
Logo Integrity
Detects distortion, incorrect aspect ratios, and Safe Zone violations. Returns bounding-box coordinates for each issue found — the exact pixels, not a general description.
T
Brand Tone Check
Uses Gemini 3 Pro's reasoning to evaluate whether imagery content aligns with the brand voice, mood, and values defined in the Brand DNA vault.
V
Visual Hierarchy Audit
Pixel-precise analysis of landing page screenshots. Watcher Agents use this to identify hierarchy failures and justify optimization proposals before any change is staged.

AI-generated landing page
Once the brand research phase completes, the NanoBananaService generates a full landing page from structured JSON, not raw HTML. Each section (Hero, Proof, PAS, Offer, Spec, Social) is produced as a typed config against one of four named layout strategies: SPLIT (content alongside a visual), CLOUDS (floating card arrangement), TRIPTYCH (three-column editorial), or FORENSIC_GRID (data-heavy structured layout). An LLM will produce valid JSON against a fixed schema far more reliably than it will produce valid HTML, and switching to this approach made the landing page output far more consistent. LEARNINGS

_______   KEY FINDINGS

Measuring success.

"The most important insight was not about AI capability. It was about trust. Users don't resist automation. They resist opacity. Once every AI decision became a visible Thought Signature on the canvas, user confidence in letting the system run autonomously increased immediately."

What worked well

The Bridge Architecture was the right call. Separating Gemini Live from the Brain completely eliminated the response latency problem that had made every earlier prototype feel broken. The voice interaction became natural enough that people who tried Beevo during the competition kept saying it felt like talking to a CMO, not a chatbot. The workspace isolation system also proved robust; multiple simultaneous demo sessions ran without state conflicts. Thought Signatures were the unexpected win: every person who saw the reasoning chain on the canvas trusted the system enough to let the Watcher Agents run on their own. Transparency, not capability, turned out to be the differentiator.

The NanoBanana approach, generating structured JSON config instead of raw HTML, was one of the best engineering decisions in the project. LLM-generated HTML is unreliable and hard to validate. Structured JSON with a fixed schema is predictable, renderable, and debuggable. The landing page sections rendered correctly on first attempt far more consistently after we switched to this approach.

What I would do differently

Persistent storage was the most painful gap in the current implementation. Cloud Run's ephemeral disk means workspace data is lost on container restart. For the hackathon timeline this was acceptable, but for a production system it's critical. The architecture needs Cloud SQL or Cloud Storage with proper persistence before real brands can rely on it. I would build this from day one rather than retrofitting it later.

Puppeteer's reliability on Cloud Run was inconsistent. Google Images IP blocking affected the logo inspiration scraping more than expected, even with the stealth plugin. The fallback to Search Grounding handled it, but a more robust design would integrate a dedicated screenshot API like Screenshotone or Browserless rather than running Chrome inside the application container.

Finally, I would build the A/B testing framework earlier. The Watcher Agents currently propose changes sequentially. True multivariate testing, deploying two landing page variants simultaneously and measuring which performs better, would make the optimization loop dramatically more powerful and would be the natural next step for what Beevo does.
