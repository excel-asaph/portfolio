I'm a software engineer currently based in Kigali, Rwanda. I build backend systems, ML pipelines, and full-stack applications. Most of what I work on sits at the intersection of real-world problems and intelligent software, the kind of products that change how people actually do things.

Most of the things I've built started with frustration. QwikAid came from watching emergency response fail the people who needed it most. Life Prescriber came from how unreliable medication adherence was for patients without consistent reminders. I build because the problems are real, and I find it hard to walk past a broken system without thinking about how to fix it.

Outside of work, I take long walks, ride bikes around Kigali's hills, play football with my friends on the weekends, show up at tech events at places like Cave Coffee, and spend too much time thinking about ideas I haven't built yet. Rwanda has a way of making you feel like everything is possible, which is probably not a coincidence for where I chose to be.
